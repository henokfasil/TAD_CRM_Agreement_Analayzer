Appendix A · Methodology
Research design

Comparative mapping,
made reproducible.

1

Define

A versioned codebook translates research questions into comparable agreement-level and provision-level variables.

2

Observe

PDF pages, OCR text and candidate passages remain linked to their source so every coded claim can be checked.

3

Validate

AI suggestions remain provisional until a researcher verifies the evidence, records a decision and supplies rationale.

Official outputs use validated records, a recorded corpus cutoff and transparent denominators, methods and limitations.

Unit of analysis: agreement + provisionComparative mapping · not causal evaluation
Appendix B · Technology and AI tools
Implementation stack

Open, modular and evidence-centred.

Research interface

Streamlit delivers the workflow, dashboards, review forms, comparison and presentation.

Documents and OCR

PyMuPDF extracts native PDF text; Tesseract OCR recovers scanned pages when available.

Persistent workspace

Neon PostgreSQL stores records; Neon Object Storage retains original PDFs across sessions.

AI + resilient analysis

Gemini 3.8 Flash is the semantic provider; a local evidence scan preserves provisional answers when provider access is unavailable.

Python data services

Pydantic + YAML validate schemas; Psycopg + Boto3 connect records and files.

Service foundation

FastAPI, SQLAlchemy and Alembic provide an API-ready path beyond the prototype.
